# Module 74: Electromigration (EM) Checking

## 🎯 Goal
Calculate current density in wires and estimate Mean Time To Failure (MTTF) using Black's Law.

## 🧠 Key Concept
Electromigration is the gradual displacement of metal atoms in a conductor due to the high current density of moving electrons. This can lead to voids (open circuits) or hillocks (short circuits). Designers must ensure current densities stay below limits, especially in hot or narrow wires.

## 📊 Metrics to Watch
- **MTTF Prediction:** Estimated lifetime of the interconnect.
- **Reliability Sensitivity:** Impact of temperature and width scaling.
